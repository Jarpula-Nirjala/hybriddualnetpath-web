How It Works

1. Upload MRI

Choose a brain MRI image (JPG/PNG, max 10 MB). It stays on your device.

2. AI Inference

The hybrid EfficientNetV2-S + Swin-Tiny fusion model runs in your browser with WebAssembly.

3. Explainable Results

See the prediction, class confidences, and a Grad-CAM++ heatmap of the regions that drove it.

Model Architecture

  • Local Path: EfficientNetV2-S for spatial feature extraction
  • Global Path: Swin-Tiny Transformer for contextual features
  • Fusion: Cross-Fusion Attention module
  • Explainability: Grad-CAM++ on local encoder conv layers
  • Classes: Alzheimer's (AD) · Parkinson's (PD) · Healthy Control
